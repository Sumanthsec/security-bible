## Core

**Core terms**

- **Entity** = who/what you are.
- **Identity** = who the system thinks you are.
- **Identifier** = the label/value the system uses to identify that identity.
- **Persona** = the expression of an identity, with context attributes. A role is a persona → [[iam-roles]].
- **Entitlement** = mapping of an identity/persona to an authorization (permission). Attaching a policy makes it an entitlement → [[iam-policies]].

**AuthN vs AuthZ**

- **Authentication (AuthN)** = logging in; proving you are who you say you are.
- **Authorization (AuthZ)** = being allowed to take an action.

**The 3 core AWS IAM primitives:** user (identity) · role (persona) · policy (entitlement).
→ [[iam-users]] · [[iam-roles]] · [[iam-policies]]
